Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to key questions students ask when finding hostels with Cynic.

How does Cynic protect students from fake agent scams?

Every single property agent on Cynic is vetted through national ID verification (NIN), CAC business records, and physical campus association standing. Furthermore, inspection is 100% free of charge before any commitment is made.

What is the difference between Initial Charge and Subsequent Charge?

In Nigeria student rentals, the Initial Charge includes the first year's rent plus one-time agency, legal agreement, and refundable caution deposit fees. The Subsequent Charge is the lower annual recurring rent due in year two and beyond.

Can I request a roommate match for a 2-bedroom flat or mini-flat?

Yes! When submitting student interest on any listing card, select "Me & 1 other". Cynic will pair you with a verified student in your institution who has matching study preferences.
