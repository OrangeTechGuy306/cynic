WHAT WE DO FOR NIGERIAN STUDENTS

Transparent, Safe & Scam-Free Student Housing

We link you up with a verified agent based in the University or College area you are looking to find any kind of apartment at, and help you find ideal roommates.

Service #1

Get In-Touch With Hostels Agent

Nigeria tertiary institution students have historically lost millions of Naira to fraudulent pseudo-agents advertising fake hostels online. At Cynic, every agent undergoes strict CAC, NIN, and campus association verification before listing.

  • Direct WhatsApp chat with accredited property managers
  • Physical room inspection scheduling with zero advance payment
  • Clear total package fee breakdowns (Rent, Legal, Caution)

Find Hostel Roommate For You

Renting a self-contain or mini-flat alone can strain student budgets. Cynic offers an intelligent roommate matchmaking tool that connects you with students in your institution based on study habits and preferences.
